This page aims at provide a response to prospective students, be it at the masters or at the PhD level.

## Masters

I teach in the masters in [Specialised Translation](https://corsi.unibo.it/2cycle/SpecializedTranslation) of Università di Bologna, and in particular in the [Translation and Technology curriculum](https://corsi.unibo.it/2cycle/SpecializedTranslation/course-structure-diagram/piano/2026/6826/C09/000/2026). I could eventually serve as supervisor of students working on natural language processing (and from time to time machine translation). In general those students sucessfully complete my NLP-related lessons (with very high marks).

For details about enrolling, fees and eventual scholarships, refer to the master's website and to the university's official communication channels. I can hardly help with any of these topics.

Last, but not least, I am open to consider supervising students from other masters programmes, as far as the programme itself allows it (this happened once with a [student from the MSc in CS at UniBO](https://albarron.github.io/author/mae-sosto/)). Feel free to get in touch about this.

## PhD

I am a member of the *collegio* of the [PhD programme in Translation, Interpreting, andIntercultural Studies](https://phd.unibo.it/traduzione-interpretazione-interculturalita/en). My PhD students do research mostly on NLP/Computer Science. 
For an overview of the kind of reseach that we do, which goes from core NLP (e.g., understanding why language models can perform certain tasks) to applications of NLP (e.g., LLMs for cultural heritage), go to the [Team section](https://albarron.github.io/#people) and look for both PhD students and alumni. The [publications section](https://albarron.github.io/publication/) should help too.

We are based in the city of Forlì, which is about 1hr away from Bologna by regional train. 

Students must apply direcly to the PhD programme, not to me. Follow the [2026 call for applications](https://phd.unibo.it/traduzione-interpretazione-interculturalita/en/admission) as an example. The application period typically opens in early Spring every year. Successful students start in November of the same year. 

As part of the necessary paperwork, applicants must submit a project proposal. I cannot help with that. I cannot read the proposal nor provide feedback. The only I can offer is a 30-minute informal call, upon request, to help you understand if you should apply to pursue a PhD under my supervision.

Last, but not least, I am open to consider co-supervising students from other PhD programmes (this happened already with a [student from INAOE](https://albarron.github.io/author/marco-casavantes/)). Feel free to get in touch about this.
